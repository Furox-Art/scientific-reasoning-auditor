**Subject:** Invitation: blinded expert labeling for a manuscript-screening validation pilot

Dear [CANDIDATE NAME],

I am [ORGANIZER NAME], [ROLE] at [INSTITUTION]. I am organizing a small
methodological validation pilot and would like to invite you to serve as one
of two independent expert reviewers.

The pilot asks whether a rule-based document-level screening tool identifies
two reporting features in biomedical manuscripts in agreement with independent
expert judgment. The tool, its outputs, the analysis plan and the other
reviewer's labels are withheld from reviewers until both worksheets are
submitted.

**What participation involves**

- Twelve open-access biomedical papers, about 60,000 words in total, supplied
  as plain text.
- Two yes/no judgments per paper, using a short coding manual: whether the
  supplied text contains a potentially overclaimed conclusion, and whether it
  contains in-text citations but no references section.
- Recording 24 verdicts in a provided JSON worksheet, with optional notes.
- Working alone. Do not discuss cases with the other reviewer, and do not use
  the audited tool, automated labeling systems or external literature. Judge
  only the supplied text.
- Returning the completed worksheet and a signed qualification, independence
  and confidentiality declaration by [DEADLINE, YYYY-MM-DD].

**Eligibility**

- PhD, MD or equivalent research training in a biomedical, health-science or
  adjacent empirical discipline.
- At least two years of active peer-review or research-ethics experience.
- Fluent in English.
- No authorship, employment, funding or personal relationship connected to
  any of the twelve papers. The declaration lists every paper for this check.
- No discussion of cases, labels or the tool with the other reviewer before
  both worksheets are submitted.

**What you will receive**

After your declaration is accepted, you will receive only the paper texts,
the coding manual and your personal worksheet. Do not search for the papers,
the tool, the study repository or the other reviewer's worksheet. The study
repository is public and contains material that could bias your judgment.

do not redistribute them. Your declaration and identity are kept outside the
study repository. Study files use a pseudonym, and no identifying details

withdraw before submitting your worksheet, in which case your partial
